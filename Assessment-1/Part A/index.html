Biography

My name is Peace Ogunlana. I have a Bachelor's degree in Mathematics from the University of Ibadan. I am a teacher with a strong interest in technology, problem-solving and continuous learning.

My background in Mathematics has developed my analytical and logical thinking skills. As a teacher, I have also developed strong communication, organisation and problem-solving abilities.

I have always been fascinated by technology and its ability to transform ideas into practical solutions. This interest encouraged me to begin my journey into software engineering and full-stack development.

Why I Joined the Programme

I joined the AltSchool Africa School of Engineering programme because I want to develop practical software engineering skills and build a strong foundation in full-stack development.

I want to understand how modern web applications are built, from creating user interfaces to developing the backend and working with databases. I also want to gain practical experience by working on real projects.

My Goals for the School of Engineering Programme

My goal is to leave this programme with more than just theoretical knowledge. I want to be able to take an idea, work through the problems involved, and build something that people can actually use.

  • Build a solid understanding of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and the other technologies needed for full-stack development.
  • Understand how the different parts of a web application work together, from the user interface to the backend and database.
  • Learn how to use AI tools effectively to support my development work while still understanding the code I write.
  • Build my own projects, learn from mistakes, and become more confident in solving programming problems independently.
  • Explore ways to use technology to solve practical problems, especially in education and other areas that affect everyday life.
  • Develop a portfolio of projects that demonstrates what I can do and how much I have grown throughout the programme.
  • Prepare myself for opportunities in the tech industry and continue developing my skills beyond the programme.
